Try this in class

  • Plants glowPick Color infrared, then hover a field. Healthy crops reflect strongly in near-infrared, so they turn bright red.
  • Slide a bandDrag one band's center from 400 to 2500 nm and watch its thumbnail. Water, plants and soil trade places between bright and dark.
  • Narrow itSet a width of 10 nm. The image turns grainy because less light reaches the sensor.
  • Add hazeRaise haze. Blue washes out first while the SWIR bands barely change, which is why SWIR sees through smoke.
  • Find the burnCompare the Vegetation index with the Burn index. The scar stands out in only one of them.
Spectra are simplified teaching curves, not measured library data. The scene is generated in your browser.
